Product Location

Display product location on ecommerce product page using google maps.


Let your customer know where your products are.

This module is useful if you need your customer to come to where your products are. You can give a specific location to a specific product, displayed on your product page with google maps API.



Add product location at sales and website menu.

You can add new locations on the go while creating new products.



Use location repeatedly.

The created location is saved so that it can be used repeatedly.



The location displayed on quotation and invoice

Make your customer easy to find your location just by seeing the address on quotation or invoice.
